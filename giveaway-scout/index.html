Scouting tips

  • Smaller giveaways (brand Instagram posts, niche blogs, local shops) have far fewer entrants than big national sweepstakes.
  • Log a giveaway in the Tracker the moment you find it — deadlines are easy to miss.
  • Mark daily-entry giveaways as "daily" so they show up at the top every day.
  • Use a separate email address for entries to keep your main inbox clean.
  • Run anything unfamiliar through the Vet tab before sharing personal details.
