About

From street art in Delhi to office towers in Berlin

My path started with a B.Arch in New Delhi and a dissertation on how street art reshapes public space — work that put me on scaffolding with muralists as often as at a drafting table. An MA in Interior Design at Berlin International followed, then practice across Morphogenesis, AVA Studio and my own studio, The Lazy Owl, before I moved into workplace strategy. In July 2024 I became Managing Director at Sieber GmbH, where I lead office design, relocation planning and "New Ways of Working" projects for clients including Deutsche Bahn and New Work SE.

"Shift your perspective from top-down aerial architectural plans to eye-level human experiences — using height, colour and materiality to define spaces." On spatial design
