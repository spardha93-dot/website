Architecture & interior projects

Design work, academic research and creative exploration from studios in New Delhi and Berlin.

Design

Arthalle X — Makers' Market

Contemporizing Arminius Markthalle, a historic Berlin market hall, by reprogramming it into a Makers' Market for local art and design — inserting a bridge structure and enclosed exhibit hall within the preserved 1891 building.

Berlin, Germany·Master Thesis, MA Interior Design, 2020
Design

Haus der Mitwirkung

A proposal reworking the former Haus der Statistik at Alexanderplatz into a "House of Participation" — spaces for co-working, seminars, exhibits and meetings shared between BIM, government and the public.

Berlin, Germany·Design Project, MA Interior Design, 2019
Design

Uhrwerk Pop-Up

A pop-up store at KaDeWe for Berlin watch brand Uhrwerk — its first physical touchpoint. A corridor of backlit frames shifts colour from day to night, staging the brand's "anything can happen in Berlin" story.

Berlin, Germany·Charrette, MA Interior Design, 2019
Design

The BI Deck

A temporary canal-side deck introduced to a brick-clad university building that otherwise ignored the water across the street — giving students an outdoor leisure space in steel and neutral timber.

Berlin, Germany·Charrette, MA Interior Design, 2019
Design

Urban Regeneration Proposal

Adaptive reuse of the derelict Rajghat Power Plant into an Urban Arts Centre — its coal-crusher tower and chimney restored as viewing platforms, connecting Delhi to a recreational Yamuna riverfront.

New Delhi, India·Bachelor Thesis, B.Arch, 2015
Design

Boxfit Fitness

A kickboxing studio designed with grunge interiors for a "night-club gym" energy — training zone, café, personal-training area, fighting ring, and a custom shelving storage and display.

New Delhi, India·Interior Architect, The Lazy Owl, 2018

Working on Lodhi Art District, Delhi, for the St+art India Foundation. Twelve artists captured walls across the district in their signature styles — a mural of Rani Laxmi Bai went up on a residence society wall in a pop-art style, painted alongside street artist Lady Aiko.

A commissioned art installation for a women's hostel wall at Majnu Ka Tila, Delhi — a woman's face strung from wire and thread, evoking a message of women's empowerment. The frame and face were built from upcycled and salvaged materials found on site.

New Delhi, India·The Lazy Owl, 2018
