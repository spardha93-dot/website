Architecture projects

A collection of built and unbuilt work across residential, cultural, commercial, and multi-family typologies — organized loosely around material honesty and a light touch on the site.

Kenton, OR·2022
Commercial

Adaptive reuse of a 1920s ironworks into a year-round market hall, retaining the original crane rail as a lighting spine.

St. Johns, OR·2021

Milwaukie, OR·2020
Cultural

Residential
