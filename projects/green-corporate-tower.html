Green Corporate Tower

New Delhi, India·2017

A G+12 storey office tower and art gallery designed for an art-enthusiast industrialist, planned to reach GRIHA gold and 5-star LEED certification. The brief combined the demands of a working corporate headquarters — executive floors, meeting suites and staff amenities — with a public-facing gallery that could exhibit the client's personal art collection, so the building reads as a piece of civic architecture as much as a place of business.

Executive floors were planned around efficient, daylit workstation layouts with private offices and breakout meeting rooms at the core. A double-height lounge breaks up the tower's working floors as an informal space for staff and visitors, while the ground-level gallery reception gives the art collection its own dedicated entrance and circulation, separate from daily office traffic.
