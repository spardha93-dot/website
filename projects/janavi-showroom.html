Hospitality

Janavi Showroom

New Delhi, India·2017

Janavi, a luxury textile and cashmere brand, commissioned a second showroom at The Chanakya, Delhi, to match the standard set by its flagship store. The design needed to carry the brand's high-end retail experience — rich materials, considered lighting and a sense of occasion — into a smaller footprint without feeling compressed.

A faux fireplace and terrazzo flooring anchor the main showroom floor, giving the space a residential warmth alongside the polish expected of a luxury retail environment. Dressing areas were detailed with the same material language — mirror work set against the fireplace, and custom shelving built to display folded textiles as part of the room's decor rather than as storage.
