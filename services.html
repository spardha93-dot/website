What I offer

Interior design consulting for workplace and hospitality projects

I work with corporates and hospitality brands as an independent consultant — either leading a project end to end, or advising an in-house team at a specific stage.

Workplace & Corporate Offices

  • Space planning & workplace strategy
  • Office fit-out design & relocation planning
  • Furniture, material & finish specification
  • Design review & advisory for in-house teams

Hospitality & Retail

  • Restaurant, café & bar interiors
  • Retail & showroom design
  • Brand-led concept development
  • Renovation & retrofit of existing spaces

How a project runs

Discovery & Brief

Understanding how your business, team or brand actually operates, and translating that into a clear design brief and budget.

Concept & Space Planning

Early layouts and concept direction — tested against how the space will really be used, day to day.

Design Development

Materials, finishes, furniture and detailing developed into drawings ready for pricing and construction.

Delivery & Handover

Coordination through fit-out, with site visits and reviews to see the design through to a finished space.
