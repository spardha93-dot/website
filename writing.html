Research, publications & press

Writing

Academic research and design writing alongside the practice — from a bachelor's dissertation on street art to a research paper on post-pandemic office design, plus freelance and press features.

2017

LOT-EK: Objects + Operations

Contributed research and writing to LOT-EK's monograph documenting the studio's container-based architecture and installation work.

2020

Workplace Dynamics in a Hybrid World

Research paper for the Master's Research Colloquium at Berlin International, investigating how corporate environments adapt to post-pandemic remote work, desk-sharing and hybrid office models.

2014

Connecting the City to the Yamuna via Urban Art

Bachelor's thesis proposing riverfront rejuvenation through an arts centre built from the adaptive reuse of the Rajghat Power Plant, New Delhi.

2015

Public Art Study and Project

Bachelor's dissertation researching how street art transforms a space — its quality, social structure, activity and behavioural patterns — through before-and-after activity mapping of a mural in Khirkee urban village.

2013

Architecture of Cologne

Unpublished design-studio documentation: a photographic study of vernacular and civic architecture across Cologne, Germany.

2012

Purification

An architectural documentation study of a vernacular settlement in the pilgrimage city of Puri, Orissa.

2017–18

Freelance writing for Little Black Book (LBB) India

Early-career articles on design, craft and culture, written alongside running The Lazy Owl.

2021

Guest critic, "Reincarnating Cities" studio

Invited guest critic at the School of Building Science and Technology, CEPT University, for a studio on adaptive reuse and urban regeneration.

2020

Berlin International logo design contest

Received an honorable mention from the jury for a submission to redesign the Berlin International Alumni Association's official logo.
